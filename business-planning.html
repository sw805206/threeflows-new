The Way We See It

Most founders want to get to the doing. Reviewing assumptions, checking run rates, arguing about productivity numbers — it feels like homework standing between you and the actual business.

It isn’t. Every one of those numbers is an input to a single output: your cashflow projection. Because cash is king — or rather, it’s what kills small businesses.

So we work the assumptions until they hold. We test the market, we test the customer, and we carry both into a budget, a P&L, and cashflow scenarios that show you your floor as clearly as your ceiling. You will see the reasoning behind every figure, so you are fully prepared even before everything starts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the business planning engagement take?
It depends on the package. Starter reports take 1–2 business days; a standard engagement runs 1–2 weeks; a comprehensive one, 2–3 weeks. We agree the timeline together at the discovery call before the clock starts.
How can I reduce the cost and turnaround time?
Come prepared. The more you’ve done up front — competitor research, product specs, a rough cost target, your target customer — the less time we spend on discovery and the faster we reach the analysis that matters.
What if my idea doesn’t pass validation?
That’s a success — you’ve saved yourself a much more expensive mistake. We walk you through exactly what the data shows and what would need to change to make the idea viable.
How can you project cashflow when I have no history?
We start from industry standards and build a best case and a worst case around them. What you’ll find matters most is your sales run rate — that’s where we spend the bulk of the discussion, because nearly everything else in the model depends on it.
I already have a business plan. Can you review it instead of starting over?
Yes, and it’s usually the shorter engagement. We pressure-test what you’ve built rather than rebuild it: where each assumption came from, whether the numbers still connect to one another, and what changes if the market has moved since you wrote it. You get back a marked-up plan and a clear list of what to fix, not a replacement.
